# Painted combat feedback — option A

Status: **Accepted and implemented (2026-11-05)**. The user accepted option A
as part of the completed SE02 slice.
See the [design and checks](../../combat_feedback_design.md),
[approved concept](../combat-feedback-concepts/a-painted-splash.png) and
[source artwork/prompt](../../../assets/effects/combat-feedback/README.md).

- [World feedback animation](world-feedback.gif): saved Poison Spray uses charcoal;
  Scorching Ray deals 3 damage with crimson backing and produces two neutral misses.
- [Portrait feedback animation](portrait-feedback.gif): an actual goblin weapon
  hit deals 9 damage to Alden; other portraits remain unaffected.
- [Saved outcome at 120 ms](saved-120.png): native 1920×1080 capture.
- [Portrait hit at 120 ms](portrait-hit-120.png): native 1920×1080 capture.

The result appears instantly, holds for 400 ms, then fades over 240 ms. Only damage
numerals tremble briefly (100 ms, under two native pixels); the backdrop is steady.
Prepared rounded blood/ink shapes replace the jagged starburst. Healing/buff/spell
portrait shimmers keep their existing artwork and number-only treatment.

GIFs use native-scale crops, sampled every 20 ms from 0–1,320 ms with a 600 ms
final-frame hold. Timestamps and panel headings are review annotations. Poison
and the portrait sequence begin at impact; Scorching Ray impacts at 297 ms after
cast. GIF palette quantization limits colour assessment; PNGs preserve native
output. All outcomes are generated by production commands/rules, seed 5, with
production art. Captures use hidden application-owned targets, without desktop
input or focus automation. Immunity classification is CPU-tested but not separately
captured; interactive feel and cross-device appearance remain unverified.

To reproduce after building `blobber` and `blobber_world_integration_gpu_tests`,
create an owned run and execute one case at a time:

```sh
python3 tools/agent_artifacts.py create
nice -n 15 ionice -c 3 ./cmake-build-debug/blobber_world_integration_gpu_tests \
  cmake-build-debug/assets RUN/poison --feedback-review poison-spray
```

Use `scorching-ray` or `portrait-weapon` for the other cases, each in a new/empty
output directory. The runner checks OpenGL errors and warmed presentation
allocations. Six review files are retained here (four accepted painted-feedback references and
two new enemy-lunge references), plus two original concept boards
in the linked gallery. Superseded iterations and temporary captures/logs were
removed after promotion; original artwork and prompt remain durable inputs.


## Enemy melee lunge

Status: **Ready for review (2026-11-06)**. The earlier painted-feedback acceptance
is unchanged. See the [design and focused checks](../../combat_feedback_design.md#enemy-melee-lunge).

- [Four-creature motion comparison](enemy-melee.gif): goblin, giant rat, bugbear and
  zombie lunges peak at their actual impact and snap back within 120ms.
- [Goblin at impact, native resolution](enemy-melee-impact.png): actual 1920×1080
  output for checking sprite registration, silhouette, floor and shadow.

Twenty-one frames sample -160 through +240ms around each impact at 20ms intervals,
followed by a 600ms hold. The four reduced world crops have review-only labels; the
native PNG has no annotations. Production rules/art, seed 5 and ordinary Defend
commands produce the real enemy attacks. All four rolls miss; movement is cosmetic
and independent of hit outcome. Picking, unchanged grid/subcell positions, exactly
one impact and warmed engine-owned allocation/GL checks pass. No OS input or focus
control was used. Reproduce with an owned run:

```sh
nice -n 15 ionice -c 3 ./cmake-build-debug/blobber_world_integration_gpu_tests \
  cmake-build-debug/assets RUN/melee --enemy-melee-review
```

The directory must be new or empty. Temporary captures and logs were cleaned.
The six-file gallery plus two concept boards retain distinct accepted style references
and the new multi-species motion/native-resolution comparison; concept retention
does not imply acceptance of the new lunge. Interactive feel remains for user review.
